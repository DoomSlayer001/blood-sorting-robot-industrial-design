# Material Plan

## Base Plate And Mounting Plates

- Material: 6061-T6 aluminum alloy.
- Reason: good machinability, dimensional stability, moderate strength, and common availability.
- Surface treatment: anodizing or clear protective finish.

## Tube Racks

- Material: POM or PC.
- Reason: POM provides wear resistance and machinability; PC provides visual inspection and impact resistance.
- Surface treatment: deburring and edge rounding.

## Gripper Soft Pads

- Material: silicone or TPU.
- Reason: protects test tubes, increases friction, and reduces glass/plastic tube stress.
- Surface treatment: molded or bonded pad surface with controlled grip contact.

## Protective Cover

- Material: transparent PC.
- Reason: impact resistance, visibility, and common use in laboratory protective enclosures.
- Surface treatment: clear sheet finish, rounded edges.

## Fasteners

- Material: 304 stainless steel.
- Reason: corrosion resistance and clean laboratory appearance.

## Notes

All custom parts shall later include drawing-level material callouts, datum references, tolerance level, surface treatment, and manufacturing method.
