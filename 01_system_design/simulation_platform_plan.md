# Simulation Platform Plan

## SolidWorks

SolidWorks is the primary mechanical engineering platform. It is used for industrial CAD modeling, assembly structure, material assignment, engineering drawings, interference checks, and manufacturing exports.

## MATLAB/Simulink

MATLAB/Simulink is the primary control simulation platform. It is used for kinematics, rack coordinate generation, trajectory planning, PID control, equivalent dynamics, motor sizing support, tracking error metrics, and plots.

## Isaac Sim

Isaac Sim is the visual demonstration platform. It is used for imported CAD visualization, materials, lighting, cameras, and presentation-quality sorting animations.

## Gazebo

Gazebo is not used in this project. It is intentionally excluded to keep the toolchain focused on SolidWorks, MATLAB/Simulink, Isaac Sim, and GitHub.
