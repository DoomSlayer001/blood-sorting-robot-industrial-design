# First Rough Assembly Manual Checklist

## 1. Structure Check

- [ ] Left and right Y axes are parallel.
- [ ] Left and right Y-axis spacing is approximately 720 mm.
- [ ] X axis spans the left and right Y axes.
- [ ] X-axis height is sufficient for the 100 mm tube, Z module, gripper, and safe travel.
- [ ] Z module is positioned near the X-axis carriage region.
- [ ] Z module points vertically.
- [ ] Gripper points downward.
- [ ] Gripper centerline is aligned with the Z-axis centerline.

## 2. Sample Area Check

- [ ] Mixed input rack is in the rear-side area.
- [ ] Four output bins form a 2 x 2 arrangement.
- [ ] Manual review bin is located near a front-side corner or output edge.
- [ ] Every bin is inside the expected gripper reachable area.
- [ ] There is enough spacing for gripper approach and held-tube clearance.
- [ ] 75 mm and 100 mm tubes clear neighboring bin walls at safe height.

## 3. Scanning Area Check

- [ ] Barcode scanner faces the tube label side.
- [ ] Photoelectric sensor can detect the tube body at the scan station.
- [ ] Scan station does not block gripper travel.
- [ ] Scanner bracket will not interfere with the Z axis or gripper.
- [ ] Photoelectric sensor bracket has space for adjustment.
- [ ] Cable exits from scanner and sensor are not blocked.
- [ ] Dedicated scanner and photoelectric brackets are needed in later custom-part design.

## 4. Motion Space Check

- [ ] Gripper safe height is above the 100 mm tube and cap.
- [ ] Gripper descent does not collide with rack/bin edges.
- [ ] Z module does not collide with gantry beam.
- [ ] Z module does not collide with scanner/sensor hardware.
- [ ] Cable chain sweep does not hit brackets, racks, or tube bins.
- [ ] Limit switches can be placed with reachable trigger targets.
- [ ] Emergency stop is reachable by an operator.

## 5. Screenshot Requirements

Capture the following after rough assembly creation:

- [ ] overall top view.
- [ ] front view.
- [ ] side view.
- [ ] gripper-to-tube local view.
- [ ] scanning station local view.
- [ ] output bin area local view.
- [ ] cable chain and moving-axis local view.

## 6. Decision Before Stage 4B

- [ ] Confirm whether coordinate placement is acceptable for macro generation.
- [ ] Confirm whether missing base plate/control box/scan reference CAD should remain as coordinate notes or be modeled in Stage 4E.
- [ ] Confirm whether Y-axis sync mechanism is shown as a note or temporary placeholder in the rough assembly.
