# Control Box Placeholder Parameters

## Dimensions

- File: `control_box_placeholder_160x120x80.step`
- Size: 160 mm x 120 mm x 80 mm

## Purpose

Control box envelope placeholder for the first rough layout. It reserves space for future controller, power supply, motor drivers, wiring terminals, and panel hardware.

## Material Suggestion

- sheet metal enclosure or plastic industrial enclosure

## Placeholder Status

Placeholder only. It does not include internal PLC, power, motor driver, wiring, connector, or thermal details.

## Parameters To Freeze Later

- selected commercial enclosure or custom sheet-metal size
- cable entry direction
- emergency stop and panel switch placement
- internal electrical layout and heat dissipation
- mounting holes

## Drawing Requirement

Not required for the placeholder. Required if converted to a custom enclosure or mounting plate.
