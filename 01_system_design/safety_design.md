# Safety Design

## Emergency Stop

The robot shall include a visible emergency stop button on the front or front-side operator area. It should cut actuator power through a safety-rated circuit in a production design.

## Limit Switches

Each linear axis shall include home and travel-limit detection. Software limits shall be aligned with physical travel and verified during commissioning.

## Software Limits

The motion controller shall enforce X/Y/Z travel boundaries, maximum velocity, maximum acceleration, and forbidden zones around rack walls and enclosure boundaries.

## Protective Cover

A transparent PC protective cover shall reduce contact risk with moving axes and sample handling components while preserving visual inspection.

## Safe Height

Trajectory planning shall include a safe Z height above tube caps and rack walls before X/Y movement between rack positions.

## Gripper Soft Pads

Silicone or TPU pads shall reduce tube damage risk and improve gripping reliability.

## Cable Chain

Moving cables shall be routed through drag chains to prevent snagging, fatigue, and interference with moving parts.

## Production Review

Final production design requires professional safety review, electrical design validation, pinch-point assessment, and manufacturability review.
